# Architecture decisions

Record durable decisions that constrain future changes. Append entries; do not rewrite history.

## ADR template

### ADR-NNN: Title
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded
- **Context:** What evidence and constraints forced a decision?
- **Decision:** What architecture or contract is chosen?
- **Consequences:** Benefits, costs, compatibility, memory/timing, and operational impact.
- **Validation:** Tests, hardware evidence, manifest/release checks.
- **Rollback/Supersedes:** How to revert, or which ADR this replaces.
